# Acknowledgements

NullPerator grows from the PicoTracker codebase and the broader LittleGPTracker
(LGPT) tracker tradition. We are grateful to their authors and contributors,
and to the maintainers of the libraries and platform components used by this
firmware and manual.

The structure and selected explanations in this manual were rewritten from the
[PicoTracker Pico Edition manual](https://github.com/xiphonics/picoTracker/tree/98a96afe0760c8740eaa8c62c797d6c8ae35b1fd/usermanual/pico-edition/content/pages),
pinned at upstream commit `98a96afe0760c8740eaa8c62c797d6c8ae35b1fd`.
The original UI screenshots, keypad photographs, templates, and font files are
not redistributed; this site captures its interface images from the running
NullPerator application.

xiphonics. The full redistribution terms are preserved in the documentation
repository's third-party notice.

Licenses, copyright attributions, and component-specific notices are listed in
the repository's
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](https://github.com/203Null/nullperator-wiki/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
