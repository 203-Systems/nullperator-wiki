# NullPerator Manual

NullPerator is an eight-track music workstation for building complete songs
from small, reusable patterns. You can use it on **NullPerator hardware**,
**Web**, or **iOS**.

Like other trackers, **time moves from top to bottom** while **tracks run from
left to right**. The play cursor follows the rows downward as the music plays.

## How a song fits together

You only need four ideas to make your first song:

<MusicFlow />

**Song** arranges chains across the eight tracks. A **Chain** plays several
phrases in order, such as a verse. A **Phrase** is a short pattern, such as one
bar of drums. An **Instrument** gives the notes in that phrase their sound.
Select any step in the diagram to open its guide.

Tables and Grooves add more control later. You do not need them for the
[first-sound walkthrough](./start-here/quick-start).

This manual covers **NullPerator 0.1**.
