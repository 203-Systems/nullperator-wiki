# Quick start

This walkthrough starts with an empty project and uses the built-in OPAL synth,
so it does not need a sample or demo file.

## Before you start

- Make sure audio is ready on your device. See [Use NullPerator anywhere](./platforms)
  if this is your first session.
- Save any work you want to keep; the first step replaces the current project.
- Find **Shift**, **Enter**, **Play**, and the directions in
  [Controls at a glance](../controls/buttons).

On Web, choose **Enable sound** before using Enter or Play for the first time.

In the steps below, “tap” means press and release; “hold” means keep the button
down.

## 1. Open a clean project

If an existing song opens on Song, hold Shift and tap Up to open **Project**.
Place the cursor on **Name**, use Left or Right to select **New** in the bottom
bar, and press <Keycap>Enter</Keycap>. If NullPerator warns that unsaved changes
will be lost, choose **Yes** and press Enter again.

After choosing New, NullPerator returns to **Song** automatically. Put the
cursor on row `00`, track `T1`.

<InterfaceShot src="img/screens/song-empty.png" alt="Empty Song view with row numbers on the left and eight tracks across the screen">
  Song arranges music across eight tracks. Start at row 00 under T1.
</InterfaceShot>

## 2. Make the first pattern

1. Tap <Keycap>Enter</Keycap>. The Song cell becomes `00`.
2. Hold <Keycap>Shift</Keycap> and tap <Keycap>Right</Keycap> to open **Chain 00**.
3. At Chain row `00`, tap <Keycap>Enter</Keycap>. The `PH` cell becomes `00`.
4. Hold Shift and tap Right again to open **Phrase 00**.

<InterfaceShot src="img/screens/chain.png" alt="Chain view with PH and TR columns">
  PH chooses a phrase. Leave TR at 0 for now.
</InterfaceShot>

## 3. Add a note

Keep the cursor on `NOTE`, row `00`, and tap <Keycap>Enter</Keycap>. The row now
shows note `C3` and instrument `I00`. It is normal to hear nothing yet: the
instrument slot does not have a sound until the next step.

<InterfaceShot src="img/screens/phrase-note.png" alt="Phrase row 00 showing note C3 and instrument I00">
  C3 is the pitch. I00, immediately to its right, chooses Instrument 00.
</InterfaceShot>

## 4. Give it a sound

1. Hold Shift and tap Right to open **Instrument 00**.
2. Tap <Keycap>Down</Keycap> once to move from **Name** to **Type**.
3. Tap <Keycap>Left</Keycap> once. The type wraps from `NONE` to `OPAL`.
4. Hold Shift and tap Left to return to Phrase.

<InterfaceShot src="img/screens/instrument-opal.png" alt="Instrument 00 with Type set to OPAL">
  When Type reads OPAL and the settings appear, Instrument 00 is ready.
</InterfaceShot>

## 5. Hear and play it

Back on Phrase row `00`, hold <Keycap>Enter</Keycap> while the cursor is on the
`C3` note. You should hear the note immediately; release Enter to stop the
preview.

Tap <Keycap>Play</Keycap> to hear the pattern in sequence. A filled triangle in
the top bar means playback is active. Tap Play again to stop.

If there is no sound, check these three things:

- Audio is ready on your device.
- The Phrase row shows both `C3` and `I00`.
- Instrument 00 shows `TYPE OPAL`, not `NONE`.

## 6. Name and save it

<InterfaceShot src="img/screens/project-name.png" alt="Project page with the Name row selected">
  Name actions appear in the bottom bar: New, Load, Save, and Rename.
</InterfaceShot>

1. Hold Shift and tap Left to go from Phrase to Chain.
2. Hold Shift and tap Left again to go from Chain to Song.
3. Hold Shift and tap Up to open Project.
4. Focus **Name**, choose **Rename** in the bottom bar, and press Enter.
5. Enter a non-empty name and choose **Save**.
6. Back on Project, choose **Save** in the Name bottom bar.

NullPerator also autosaves when it is safe. Before closing the app, powering
off, or removing storage, stop playback and wait for the saving mark in the
top-right corner to disappear. Use **Save** whenever you want a known
checkpoint.
